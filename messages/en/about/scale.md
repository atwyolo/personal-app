questionTitle: Teams and scale
questionMessage: What kind of teams have you worked in?
answerTitle: From small teams to large multi-team projects.
---

The smallest team I worked in had four people: a business analyst, a backend developer, a QA engineer, and me, responsible for the frontend. The largest was at [FreshDirect](/experience#freshdirect), with four frontend teams of 5–6 engineers each and two tech leads who coordinated the work across all teams. At Sony, I led one of two frontend teams of four engineers: I planned the work for the whole team, took part in planning features and development phases with project management, reviewed my teammates’ code, and kept the part of the codebase my team owned in good technical shape.
